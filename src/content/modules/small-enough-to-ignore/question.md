**(i)** Show that, for small $\alpha$,

$$
\cos(\theta+\alpha)-\cos\theta\approx-\alpha\sin\theta-\tfrac12\alpha^{2}\cos\theta .
$$

Hence find

$$
\lim_{\alpha\to0}\frac{\sin(\theta+\alpha)-\sin\theta}{\cos(\theta+\alpha)-\cos\theta}
$$

when $\sin\theta\neq0$, and describe what happens to the same ratio as
$\alpha\to0$ when $\sin\theta=0$.

**(ii)** A circle $C_1$ of radius $a$ rolls anticlockwise, without slipping,
around the outside of a fixed circle $C_2$ centred at the origin $O$ with radius
$(n-1)a$, where $n>2$ is an integer. A point $P$ is fixed on $C_1$. At the
start, the centre of $C_1$ is at $(na,0)$ and $P$ is at $\bigl((n+1)a,0\bigr)$.

**(a)** Let $Q$ be the point where the circles touch, and let $\theta$ be the
anticlockwise angle from the positive $x$-axis to $OQ$. Show that $P$ has
$x$-coordinate $x(\theta)=a(n\cos\theta+\cos n\theta)$, and find $y(\theta)$.

**(b)** Find the values of $\theta$ for which $OP=(n-1)a$.

**(c)** Let $\theta_0=\dfrac{\pi}{n-1}$. Find

$$
\lim_{\alpha\to0}\frac{y(\theta_0+\alpha)-y(\theta_0)}{x(\theta_0+\alpha)-x(\theta_0)},
$$

and deduce that the tangent to the path of $P$ at $\theta=\theta_0$ is
parallel to $OP$.
